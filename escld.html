01Overview

An independent association, run by students.

ESCLD promotes the study and understanding of law, diplomacy and European affairs, and connects young people from different backgrounds and disciplines.

Its work takes the form of publications, debates, meetings and academic cooperation projects, open to students, early-career researchers and academic partners.
