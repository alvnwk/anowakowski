01 / About us

A shared vision.
Distinct ambitions.

nova corporate is an independent organisation bringing together the strategic vision and development of Auralys and the European Student Council for Law and Diplomacy.

Built around a long-term ambition, nova corporate provides a common strategic framework to support its initiatives, encourage collaboration, foster innovation and explore new opportunities. Its approach combines strategic direction, organisational development and the creation of new ventures across different fields.
