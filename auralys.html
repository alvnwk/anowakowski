(01) Overview

The music social network to discover, rate, rank and share the tracks you love with your friends.

Auralys turns listening into something you share. Every track can be reviewed, ranked and passed on, so taste becomes something you build with the people around you.

The app is built around three habits: saying what you think of a song, keeping your own ranking up to date, and finding something new every day.
